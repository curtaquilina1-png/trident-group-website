# Trident Group Solutions Website Design

## Purpose

Create a fast, mobile-first, multi-page website for Trident Group Solutions in Malta that presents its two divisions, attracts professional repeat clients as well as property owners, and collects structured project enquiries.

## Architecture

The first release is a dependency-free static site suitable for GitHub Pages. Shared CSS and JavaScript provide navigation, portfolio filtering, conditional enquiry fields, privacy-friendly cookie preferences, and progressively enhanced form behaviour. Individual HTML documents provide unique metadata and crawlable page copy.

## Content and verification

Only services confirmed by the owner are included. Unknown legal, contact, licensing, insurance, business-hour, social, and response-time details remain visibly marked with the exact placeholders supplied by the owner. Real project images and logos will be integrated after files and publication consent are supplied; the site never substitutes stock or generated construction imagery.

## Visual direction

Black, off-white, stone grey, and a restrained bronze accent. Square geometry, strong typography, generous whitespace, visible focus states, and no gradients, glossy effects, hero carousel, or decorative rounded-card overload.

## Pages

Home; Rockscape Solutions; Construction Solutions; Our Work; For Periti and Contractors; About Trident; Equipment and Access; Request a Quote; Contact; Insights; Privacy; Cookies; Website Terms; plus dedicated SEO pages for excavation, mini excavation and difficult access, demolition, trenching and drainage preparation, concrete breaking and controlled removal, interior excavation and garage lowering, and structural alterations.

## Enquiry flow

Visitors select their role first. Professional-only fields appear for periti, architects, developers, and contractors. The form includes all requested project fields and explains that attachments and automated confirmation require the launch form service to be connected. The static preview validates input and produces a reference locally without transmitting personal information.

## Launch boundary

Before public launch, replace all placeholders, add approved real logos and photographs, configure the form delivery/upload/spam service, configure analytics with consent, test notifications, review legal copy, validate structured data, and connect the custom domain.
